# Research Paper Analyzer & Summary

## Category
Learning & Education

## Description
Analyzes academic papers and creates comprehensive summaries with key findings, methodology, and practical implications.

## Prompt
```
Analyze and summarize the following research paper:

Paper Title: [Full title]
Authors: [Author names]
Publication: [Journal/Conference]
Year: [Publication year]
Field: [Academic field]

[Paste paper abstract or key sections, or provide DOI/URL]

Please provide:

1. EXECUTIVE SUMMARY (2-3 paragraphs)
   - Main research question
   - Key findings
   - Significance

2. DETAILED BREAKDOWN
   - Background & motivation
   - Research methodology
   - Data and experiments
   - Results and analysis
   - Limitations acknowledged

3. KEY CONTRIBUTIONS
   - Novel insights
   - Advancement over previous work
   - Practical applications

4. CRITICAL ANALYSIS
   - Strengths of the research
   - Potential weaknesses or limitations
   - Questions for further investigation

5. PRACTICAL IMPLICATIONS
   - Real-world applications
   - Who would benefit from this research
   - How to apply these findings

6. RELATED WORK & FURTHER READING
   - Connected research areas
   - Papers cited most relevant
   - Suggested next papers to read

Make it accessible to someone learning in this field.
```

## Usage Guide
1. Read the abstract first
2. Review the AI summary
3. Read full paper sections highlighted as important
4. Note key citations
5. Consider practical applications
6. Compare with your understanding
7. Explore related papers

## Example Output
A comprehensive paper analysis with clear summary, methodology breakdown, critical insights, and connections to broader research landscape.

## Tips
- Start with executive summary
- Focus on methodology to assess rigor
- Note limitations carefully
- Look for replication studies
- Check citation count and impact
- Read cited papers for context
- Apply critical thinking
- Make connections to your work
