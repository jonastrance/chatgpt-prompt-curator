# Study Plan & Exam Preparation Guide

## Category
Learning & Education

## Description
Creates strategic study plans and exam preparation schedules optimized for retention, understanding, and test performance.

## Prompt
```
Create a comprehensive study plan for:

Exam/Test: [Name and type]
Exam Date: [When is the exam]
Time Available: [Weeks/months until exam]
Study Time Per Day: [Available hours]
Current Knowledge Level: [What you know already]

Subject Areas to Cover:
1. [Topic 1 - with importance level]
2. [Topic 2 - with importance level]
3. [Topic 3 - with importance level]

Please create:

1. STUDY SCHEDULE
   - Week-by-week breakdown
   - Daily study blocks
   - Topic rotation strategy
   - Review cycles

2. LEARNING STRATEGIES
   - Active recall techniques
   - Spaced repetition schedule
   - Practice test schedule
   - Note-taking methods

3. RESOURCE ALLOCATION
   - Time per topic based on importance
   - Weak areas focus time
   - Practice vs. learning ratio

4. MILESTONES & CHECKPOINTS
   - Weekly progress checks
   - Practice test schedule
   - Topics to master by when

5. FINAL WEEK STRATEGY
   - Review priorities
   - Practice test frequency
   - Rest and preparation

6. EXAM DAY PREPARATION
   - Last-minute review plan
   - Mental preparation
   - Logistics checklist
```

## Usage Guide
1. Start planning early (ideally 8+ weeks before exam)
2. Be realistic about available time
3. Prioritize weak areas
4. Schedule practice tests regularly
5. Build in buffer time
6. Track actual vs. planned progress
7. Adjust plan as you learn more

## Example Output
A detailed study schedule with daily tasks, strategic review cycles, practice test timing, and optimization for exam success.

## Tips
- Use spaced repetition for retention
- Test yourself frequently
- Focus on understanding, not memorizing
- Study in focused 25-50 minute blocks
- Review mistakes thoroughly
- Get adequate sleep
- Stay consistent with schedule
- Simulate exam conditions
