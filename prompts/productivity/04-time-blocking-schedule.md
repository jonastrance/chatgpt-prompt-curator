# Time Blocking Schedule Optimizer

## Category
Productivity

## Description
Creates an optimized daily schedule using time-blocking techniques, considering your energy levels, priorities, and meeting commitments.

## Prompt
```
Help me create a time-blocked schedule for my workday:

Work Hours: [e.g., 9 AM - 5 PM]
Peak Energy Time: [e.g., Morning, Afternoon, Evening]
Fixed Commitments: [List meetings and appointments with times]

Priority Tasks:
1. [Task with estimated duration]
2. [Task with estimated duration]
3. [Task with estimated duration]

Also include:
- Buffer time between tasks
- Lunch break
- Short breaks for energy management
- Email/communication check-ins

Consider my energy levels and schedule deep work during peak hours.
```

## Usage Guide
1. Identify your most productive hours
2. List all fixed commitments
3. Estimate task durations realistically
4. Block time for breaks and transitions
5. Review and adjust throughout the week

## Example Output
A detailed hour-by-hour schedule with specific time blocks for tasks, breaks, and meetings optimized for your energy patterns.

## Tips
- Schedule deep work during peak energy
- Group similar tasks together
- Include buffer time (add 25% to estimates)
- Protect your time blocks
- Review what worked at day's end
- Adjust schedule based on actual performance
