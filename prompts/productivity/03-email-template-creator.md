# Professional Email Template Creator

## Category
Productivity

## Description
Generates professional email templates for common business situations, saving time and ensuring consistent, effective communication.

## Prompt
```
Create a professional email template for the following situation:

Email Purpose: [e.g., Follow-up after meeting, Request for information, Project update]
Recipient: [e.g., Client, Manager, Team member]
Tone: [e.g., Formal, Semi-formal, Friendly professional]
Key Points to Include:
1. [Point 1]
2. [Point 2]
3. [Point 3]

Please include:
- Appropriate subject line
- Professional greeting
- Clear body structure
- Call to action
- Professional closing
```

## Usage Guide
1. Identify the email's purpose
2. Consider your relationship with the recipient
3. List all key information to include
4. Review and personalize the generated template
5. Adjust tone as needed

## Example Output
A complete email template with subject line, body, and signature that you can customize with specific details.

## Tips
- Keep emails concise and scannable
- Use bullet points for multiple items
- Include specific call-to-action
- Proofread before sending
- Save templates for recurring email types
