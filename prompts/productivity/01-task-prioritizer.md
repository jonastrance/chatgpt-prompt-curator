# Task Prioritizer Pro

## Category
Productivity

## Description
Analyzes your task list and prioritizes items using the Eisenhower Matrix (urgent/important framework) to help you focus on what truly matters.

## Prompt
```
I have the following tasks to complete. Please analyze them using the Eisenhower Matrix and categorize each as:
1. Urgent & Important (Do First)
2. Important but Not Urgent (Schedule)
3. Urgent but Not Important (Delegate)
4. Neither Urgent nor Important (Eliminate)

Also provide a recommended order and time estimate for each task.

My tasks:
[LIST YOUR TASKS HERE]

Additional context about my goals and deadlines:
[ADD CONTEXT]
```

## Usage Guide
1. List all your pending tasks in the prompt
2. Add any relevant deadlines or context
3. Review the categorization and adjust based on your specific situation
4. Focus on Quadrant 1 (Urgent & Important) first
5. Schedule time for Quadrant 2 (Important but Not Urgent) tasks

## Example Output
The AI will provide a structured breakdown showing which tasks to tackle immediately, which to schedule, and which to delegate or eliminate.

## Tips
- Be specific with your task descriptions
- Include deadlines when known
- Review and update your prioritization weekly
- Don't ignore Quadrant 2 tasks - they prevent future fires
