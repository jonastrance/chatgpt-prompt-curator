# SWOT Analysis Generator

## Category
Business Strategy

## Description
Conducts comprehensive SWOT (Strengths, Weaknesses, Opportunities, Threats) analysis for strategic business planning and decision-making.

## Prompt
```
Conduct a detailed SWOT analysis for:

Business/Project: [Name and description]
Industry: [Your industry]
Market Position: [e.g., Startup, Established player, Market leader]
Geographic Focus: [Local, National, International]

Background Context:
- Current situation: [Brief overview]
- Key competitors: [List main competitors]
- Recent changes: [Market or business changes]

Please analyze:
STRENGTHS (Internal, Positive)
- What advantages do we have?
- What do we do better than others?
- What unique resources do we possess?

WEAKNESSES (Internal, Negative)
- What could we improve?
- Where do we lack resources?
- What do competitors do better?

OPPORTUNITIES (External, Positive)
- What market trends can we leverage?
- What gaps exist in the market?
- What partnerships could we form?

THREATS (External, Negative)
- What obstacles do we face?
- What are competitors doing?
- What external factors could harm us?

Provide actionable recommendations based on this analysis.
```

## Usage Guide
1. Gather data on your business/project
2. Research competitors and market trends
3. Be honest about weaknesses
4. Involve team members for diverse perspectives
5. Create action plans from recommendations
6. Review quarterly

## Example Output
A structured SWOT analysis with detailed points in each quadrant and strategic recommendations for leveraging strengths and opportunities while addressing weaknesses and threats.

## Tips
- Be specific and realistic
- Use data to support points
- Prioritize most impactful factors
- Convert analysis into action items
- Update regularly as conditions change
- Share with stakeholders for alignment
