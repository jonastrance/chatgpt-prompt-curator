# Competitive Analysis Framework

## Category
Business Strategy

## Description
Creates comprehensive competitive analysis to understand market positioning, identify gaps, and develop winning strategies.

## Prompt
```
Create a detailed competitive analysis for:

Our Business: [Your company/product]
Industry: [Your industry]
Market Segment: [Target market]

Competitors to Analyze:
1. [Competitor 1 - Direct competitor]
2. [Competitor 2 - Direct competitor]
3. [Competitor 3 - Indirect competitor]

For each competitor, analyze:
- Products/Services offered
- Pricing strategy
- Target audience
- Market share (if known)
- Strengths and weaknesses
- Marketing approach
- Customer reviews/feedback
- Technology/platform used
- Unique selling propositions
- Distribution channels

Also provide:
- Competitive positioning map
- Market gaps and opportunities
- Our differentiation strategy
- Recommendations for competitive advantage
```

## Usage Guide
1. Identify 3-5 key competitors
2. Gather public information (websites, reviews, social media)
3. Analyze their strategies objectively
4. Identify patterns and trends
5. Find your unique positioning
6. Update analysis every 6 months

## Example Output
A comprehensive competitive analysis with detailed comparisons, positioning insights, and strategic recommendations for differentiation.

## Tips
- Use competitor websites and social media
- Read customer reviews for insights
- Track their content and marketing
- Mystery shop their products/services
- Monitor their hiring and expansion
- Look for unmet customer needs
- Focus on sustainable differentiation
