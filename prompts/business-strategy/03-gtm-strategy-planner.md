# Go-to-Market Strategy Planner

## Category
Business Strategy

## Description
Develops comprehensive go-to-market strategies for product launches, including target audience, positioning, channels, and success metrics.

## Prompt
```
Create a go-to-market strategy for:

Product/Service: [Name and description]
Target Launch Date: [When]
Market: [Geographic and demographic]
Budget: [Available resources]

Product Details:
- Key features: [List main features]
- Benefits: [Primary benefits]
- Price point: [Pricing strategy]
- Competitive advantage: [What makes it unique]

Please develop:

1. TARGET AUDIENCE
   - Primary persona(s)
   - Secondary audiences
   - Pain points we solve
   - Buying triggers

2. VALUE PROPOSITION
   - Core message
   - Differentiation
   - Proof points

3. POSITIONING STRATEGY
   - Market positioning
   - Brand messaging
   - Competitive positioning

4. CHANNELS & TACTICS
   - Marketing channels
   - Sales channels
   - Partnership opportunities
   - Content strategy

5. LAUNCH TIMELINE
   - Pre-launch activities
   - Launch day plan
   - Post-launch follow-up

6. SUCCESS METRICS
   - KPIs to track
   - Goals for first 30/60/90 days
```

## Usage Guide
1. Complete product research first
2. Validate target audience assumptions
3. Test messaging with focus groups
4. Prioritize channels by ROI potential
5. Create detailed timeline
6. Assign responsibilities
7. Track metrics religiously

## Example Output
A complete GTM strategy document with audience definitions, messaging framework, channel tactics, timeline, and measurable success criteria.

## Tips
- Start with smallest viable market
- Test positioning before full launch
- Align sales and marketing teams
- Create launch checklist
- Plan for multiple scenarios
- Build feedback loops
- Iterate based on early results
