# Business Model Canvas Creator

## Category
Business Strategy

## Description
Generates comprehensive Business Model Canvas to visualize and develop business models with all key components clearly defined.

## Prompt
```
Create a Business Model Canvas for:

Business Concept: [Describe your business idea]
Industry: [Your industry]
Stage: [Idea, Startup, Growth, Established]

Please develop all 9 building blocks:

1. CUSTOMER SEGMENTS
   - Who are our most important customers?
   - What are the different segments?

2. VALUE PROPOSITIONS
   - What value do we deliver to customers?
   - Which customer problems are we solving?
   - What needs are we satisfying?

3. CHANNELS
   - How do we reach customer segments?
   - How do customers want to be reached?
   - Distribution and communication channels

4. CUSTOMER RELATIONSHIPS
   - What type of relationship does each segment expect?
   - How do we acquire, retain, and grow customers?

5. REVENUE STREAMS
   - For what value are customers willing to pay?
   - What pricing mechanisms to use?
   - Revenue models (subscription, usage, etc.)

6. KEY RESOURCES
   - What key resources do we need?
   - Physical, intellectual, human, financial

7. KEY ACTIVITIES
   - What key activities does our value proposition require?
   - Production, problem-solving, platform/network

8. KEY PARTNERSHIPS
   - Who are our key partners/suppliers?
   - Strategic alliances, joint ventures

9. COST STRUCTURE
   - What are the most important costs?
   - Fixed costs, variable costs
   - Economies of scale or scope

Also provide recommendations for testing and validating key assumptions.
```

## Usage Guide
1. Start with value proposition
2. Identify customer segments clearly
3. Map how segments connect to value
4. Define revenue and cost structure
5. Validate assumptions with research
6. Iterate and refine regularly
7. Use for investor presentations

## Example Output
A complete Business Model Canvas with all 9 blocks filled out, interconnections explained, and validation recommendations.

## Tips
- Keep it visual and simple
- Focus on value creation
- Identify riskiest assumptions
- Test assumptions early
- Update as you learn
- Use for strategic alignment
- Compare with competitors' models
