# World-Building Framework

## Category
Creative Writing

## Description
Develops rich, consistent fictional worlds with detailed cultures, systems, history, and rules for immersive storytelling.

## Prompt
```
Create a world-building framework for:

Story Genre: [Fantasy, Sci-Fi, Alternate History, etc.]
World Type: [e.g., Another planet, Fantasy realm, Future Earth, Parallel dimension]
Technology Level: [Medieval, Modern, Futuristic, Mixed]
Magic/Special Elements: [If any]
Story Scope: [Single location, Region, Global, Multiple worlds]

Please develop:

1. PHYSICAL WORLD
   - Geography and climate
   - Key locations and regions
   - Natural resources
   - Flora and fauna
   - Day/night cycles, seasons

2. HISTORY & TIMELINE
   - Origin/creation
   - Major historical events
   - Wars, conflicts, alliances
   - How past shapes present
   - Timeline relevant to story

3. SOCIETIES & CULTURES
   - Major civilizations/groups
   - Social structures and hierarchies
   - Cultural values and beliefs
   - Customs and traditions
   - Language and communication

4. GOVERNMENT & POWER
   - Political systems
   - Power structures
   - Laws and justice
   - Military and defense
   - Inter-faction relationships

5. ECONOMY & RESOURCES
   - Currency and trade
   - Key resources and scarcity
   - Economic systems
   - Class divisions
   - Occupations and guilds

6. MAGIC/TECHNOLOGY SYSTEM
   - How it works (rules and limits)
   - Who can use it
   - Cost or consequences
   - How it's learned/acquired
   - Impact on society

7. RELIGION & BELIEFS
   - Deities or belief systems
   - Religious institutions
   - Spiritual practices
   - Afterlife concepts
   - Role in society

8. DAILY LIFE
   - Food and cuisine
   - Clothing and fashion
   - Entertainment and arts
   - Education
   - Family structures

9. CONFLICTS & TENSIONS
   - Current major conflicts
   - Underlying tensions
   - Brewing problems
   - Potential plot hooks

10. UNIQUE ELEMENTS
    - What makes this world special
    - Unexpected twists on conventions
    - Fresh ideas or concepts
```

## Usage Guide
1. Start with core concept
2. Build systems that interconnect
3. Create consistent rules
4. Document everything
5. Only reveal what serves the story
6. Show world through character experience
7. Update as you develop story

## Example Output
A comprehensive world-building document with interconnected systems, rich history, and logical consistency ready for storytelling.

## Tips
- Rules create limitations = conflict
- Make systems affect each other
- Culture shapes character behavior
- Show world through details
- Don't info-dump, reveal gradually
- Make world serve the story
- Keep a world bible document
- Consider unintended consequences
- Let world evolve organically
